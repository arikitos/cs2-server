# MVP hero roster

The 25 initial heroes are Spider-Man, Iron Man, Captain America, Thor, Hulk, Wolverine, Deadpool, Black Panther, Doctor Strange, Scarlet Witch, Vision, Ant-Man, Wasp, Captain Marvel, Hawkeye, Black Widow, Daredevil, Punisher, Ghost Rider, Storm, Cyclops, Jean Grey, Silver Surfer, Blade, and Moon Knight.

All balance values are editable in `config/heroes.json`.

Normalized interpretation: `1.0` is normal CS2 behavior, `0.5` is 50 percent, and `2.0` is 200 percent. `OutgoingDamageMultiplier` and `IncomingDamageMultiplier` are deliberately separate.
